Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a weekend hobby in a home kitchen, where our founder baked bread for friends and neighbors. The loaves became so popular that the neighborhood asked for more, and the bakery opened its doors on Maple Street. Today, we still bake the same way: by hand, with patience, and with the community in mind.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good baking starts with good ingredients. We believe in keeping it close to home.

  • Flour from regional mills that practice sustainable farming.
  • Eggs, butter, and milk from nearby family farms.
  • Seasonal fruit and honey from local growers and farmers’ markets.
